# 7mlabs brand assets

`full-logo-light.svg` and `full-logo-dark.svg` are unmodified copies from the
7mlabs brand kit's `logo/gradient-small/` collection. They contain the full
7mlabs wordmark and symbol, with theme-specific colors for small display sizes.

These are brand assets. Publishing them here does not place them under an SDK's
